# Storm Ops

Field app for Bedwell Tree Service storm deployments: who to call in a new town, nearby shops, property owners from county GIS, flagged lots with photos, worker search zones, storm damage layers, and a first-week checklist. Installs on a phone from the browser and works with no signal.

Open it at https://89calebferguson.github.io/storm-ops/ and add it to your home screen.

All data stays on the phone (IndexedDB). Use "Back Up My Data" on the Here tab to save a copy.
