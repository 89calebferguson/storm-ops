Hardest Hit Spots

What the highlighting is based on

Orange and red squares come from damage reports sent to the National Weather Service in that time window: downed power lines and poles count most, then tornadoes and survey damage points, then trees and wind damage. Your own Outage and Hardest Hit zones are drawn on top.

Gust dots are measured gusts: NWS storm reports plus today's peak gust at each weather station near you.

There is no free live feed of utility outage counts, so this shows where outages are most likely, not the exact count. For exact numbers, use the utility outage map link below the list.
